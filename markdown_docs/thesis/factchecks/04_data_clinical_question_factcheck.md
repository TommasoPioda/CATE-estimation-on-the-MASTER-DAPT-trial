## Items needing the author's attention

- **L200-206 (Contradicted).** The chapter's prose states the 99.5% Hotelling's T² threshold (25.2) flags "162 of the 4,579 patients (3.5%)" and the 99% threshold (23.2) flags "206". Both the live `02_pca_analysis.ipynb` notebook and the thesis's own embedded figure `Outliers_analysis.png` say **184** (99.5%) and **231** (99%). The χ² thresholds themselves are correct; only the patient counts/percentage in the prose are wrong, and they disagree with the chapter's own figure, not just with the code.
- **L208-212 (Contradicted, partial).** Downstream of the count error above, the claimed 3.4x/3.5x event-rate ratios and 11.1% CV-death share don't match what the (184-patient) outlier group actually shows: 3.75x for CV death, 3.08x for stroke, and a 13.6% CV-death share. Only the stroke share (11.4%) happens to match.
- **L47-49 (Not found).** No trace anywhere in the codebase or the two candidate reports of a documented "predictive screening + domain expert consultation" process behind the 63-covariate selection. Not contradicted, just unverifiable from the materials available — worth a source note or citation if one exists elsewhere (e.g. a supervisor conversation, an earlier internal document not in this repo).
- **L229-240 (reproducibility note, not a contradiction).** The Lasso-path figure's four traced coefficients match the embedded image exactly, but the *current* `05_Lasso_interactions.ipynb` cell that used to produce it now selects different columns. Not a factual error in the chapter, but the analysis isn't currently one-command reproducible from the live notebook — flagging in case the chapter is revised and someone tries to regenerate the figure.
