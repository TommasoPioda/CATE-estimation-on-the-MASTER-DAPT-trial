- **Contradicted — Figure 5.1 caption colour key (L113–115).** The actual `ROC_PR_out_of_fold.png` legend colours are: bleeding=light blue (✓), BARC 2/3/5=**navy/dark blue** (caption says green), MI=dark red (✓), cardiovascular death=**green** (caption says dark brown), stroke=**grey** (caption says light green). Only 2 of 5 colour labels are correct. The underlying AUROC/AUPR numbers in the figure are unaffected and match the tables exactly — this is purely a caption text error.
- **Partially supported — bleeding SHAP claim vs. the Random Forest panel (L183–186, Fig. 5.3).** The stated four drivers (age, oral anticoagulation, renal function, ACS presentation) describe the LR and GB panels reasonably well but not RF, whose actual top-5 features are white-cell count, platelet count, BMI, glycemia, and age — only age matches, and it ranks 5th. Also, "ACS presentation" is not itself one of the 10 listed MASTER DAPT HBR eligibility criteria (appendix lines 757–766); age, OAC-indication, anemia and thrombocytopenia are.
- **Partially supported — "bleeding predicted worst … in both treatment arms" (L271–277).** In the abbreviated arm (Table 5.3), stroke's AUROC (0.56) is lower than bleeding's (0.61), so this is only true once stroke is implicitly excluded as unreliable (a caveat established earlier in the chapter but not restated here).
- **Not found — "Chapter 1 warns against" using risk-under-actual-treatment as a decision rule (L26–28).** Full text of `01_effect_to_decision.tex` was read; no such warning appears there verbatim, though the general ATE-vs-CATE framing is present.
- **Not found — "wider literature reports… bleeding risk scores routinely achieve higher discrimination" (L282–284).** No discrimination/AUC/C-statistic values for any bleeding risk score are present anywhere in the two MASTER DAPT papers provided.
