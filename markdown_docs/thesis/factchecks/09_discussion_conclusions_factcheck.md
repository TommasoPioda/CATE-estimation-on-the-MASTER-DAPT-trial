## Items needing the author's attention (pulled to the top)

- **`06_heterogeneity_tradeoff.tex:190` vs `:200`** — Table tab:drtester reports BCF-bleeding AUTOC $p=0.04$; two lines of prose later the same chapter says "the closest is the BCF on bleeding, at $p=0.07$." Chapter 9 (09:29-32) cites the table's $0.04$, so it is self-consistent with the table but silently disagrees with Chapter 6's own prose. Reconcile which value is correct before either chapter is finalized.
- **`09:38-42`** — "two learned policies are significantly worse" immediately follows naming "the policy tree" and "the policy forest," inviting the reader to conclude both are significantly worse. Per Table tab:policy-value (`06_heterogeneity_tradeoff.tex:521-529`) and Ch6's own text (`:535-537`), the significantly-worse pair is actually **ATE sign** and **policy forest** — the policy tree's improvement CI ($[-0.015,+0.002]$) spans zero. Consider naming ATE sign explicitly to avoid the misread.
- **`09:142-144`** — Worth rewording so it's clear that Mechanisms 4/5 *do* carry a matched-$n$ random baseline (`08_guided_enrollment_feasibility.tex:433-435`); what's actually missing is a baseline at a *smaller* enrolled size to test the "fewer patients, same precision" question, which Ch9's own Further Work section (09:169-173) says has not yet been run.
