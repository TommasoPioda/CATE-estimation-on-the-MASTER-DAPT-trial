## Items needing attention

- **L47–48 (Partially supported)**: The "140 sites in 30 countries" and "February 2017 – December 2019" figures in the source (`NEJMoa2108749.md:203–210`) describe the 5,204-patient *screened* population, not the 4,579-patient *randomized* cohort the chapter attaches them to. Both cohorts share the same sites and enrollment window, so this isn't factually wrong, but the sentence structure implies the figures were measured on the 4,579 rather than inherited from the larger screened pool. Consider rephrasing to "screening was conducted across 140 sites in 30 countries between February 2017 and December 2019; of the patients screened, 4,579 were randomized."
- **L50–53 (Partially supported → borderline Contradicted)**: "standard DAPT strategy (continuation for at least 2 additional months)" understates the duration for the majority of the standard-therapy arm. Per `NEJMoa2108749.md:141–149` and `nejmoa2108749_appendix.md:823,829`, the general standard-therapy regimen is **at least 5 additional months** (6 months after the index procedure); "at least 2 additional months" applies only to the oral-anticoagulation subgroup (36.4% of patients). This should be corrected — either state the general 5-additional-month duration, or explicitly qualify the "2 additional months" figure as the OAC-subgroup exception.
