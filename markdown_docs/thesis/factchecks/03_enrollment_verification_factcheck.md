## Items needing the author's attention (pulled to the top)

- **L295–302 — Contradicted.** The chapter states the independent reference model is "fitted **and hypertuned**" on the entire cohort. The actual code (`05_online_learning_policy_comparison_exp.ipynb`, `reference_model = fit_cate(..., DEFAULT_CF_PARAMS, DEFAULT_NUISANCE_PARAMS)`) fits it with the hardcoded, **untuned default** hyperparameters — the same defaults `online_learning_utils.py` explicitly documents as "the fallback the tuned config replaces." Either the prose needs correcting ("fitted on the entire cohort" without "hypertuned"), or the code needs to actually hypertune the reference model to match the claim made in the text.
- **L32–35 — Inconsistent with Chapter 2.** Chapter 2 (`02_estimate_validate_effect.tex:337–342`) explicitly defines **two** off-diagonal trade-off quadrants where the answer is patient-dependent. Chapter 3 calls the bottom-right quadrant "the only" such quadrant for MASTER DAPT, without stating why the symmetric top-left quadrant is excluded from consideration (it may be near-empty or clinically irrelevant given the near-universal bleeding benefit of abbreviation, but the chapter doesn't say so).
- **L180–197 — Not found.** The chapter promises a specific empirical check — comparing the average uncertainty of the enrolled cohort against the random control arm, to verify Thompson/UCB sampling "genuinely explores" rather than degenerating to the score-only rule. No such comparison (table, figure, or stored statistic) was found in Chapter 8, the appendix, or the `results_mechanism4_5_bandit_duel.parquet` output (columns: `policy, regime, run, group, n, isch_rate, bleed_rate` — no uncertainty field).
