Bleeding and ischemic risk vary across patients, so the average treatment effect from a
randomized trial may not indicate the best DAPT duration for each of them. MASTER DAPT
randomized 4,579 high-bleeding-risk patients to abbreviated or prolonged DAPT. This thesis estimates and validates CATE for bleeding and
ischemic outcomes across five estimators, and tests guided enrollment
built on it. All estimators recover the trial's average bleeding benefit,
but none finds patient-level heterogeneity beyond noise, and no learned
policy beats treating everyone with abbreviated DAPT. Guided enrollment
differs from random selection but shows no clinical benefit.
